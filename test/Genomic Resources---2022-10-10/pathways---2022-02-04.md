### Plant Reactome Pathways

The Plant Reactome is a manually curated and peer-reviewed pathway database focused on metabolism and regulation.
Pathways curated in _Oryza sativa japonica_ are projected via orthology to other genomes, including sorghum. As of SorghumBase release 11, there are 351 orthology-based sorghum pathways for _S. bicolor_ BTx623 (NCBIv3) imported from the Plant Reactome.

 The view can be accessed from the search results interface from SorghumBase's front page. Thus, the [example](https://www.sorghumbase.org/genes?idList=SORBI_3006G095600) below shows the Plant Reactome Pathway viewer focused on the msd2 (Sobic.006G095600) gene. This gene functions as the lipoxygenase that catalyzes the same reaction (O2 + LINOLENATE --> 13-HPOT) in two distinct pathways: jasmonic acid biosynthesis pathway, and 13-LOX and 13-HPL pathway.
